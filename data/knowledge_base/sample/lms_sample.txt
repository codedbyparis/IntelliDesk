Title: Learning Management System Troubleshooting (Sample)

Problem: Course content or the LMS page does not load

1. Check the internet connection and reload the page.
2. Confirm that the address is the LMS address provided by the organization.
3. Try a supported, up-to-date browser and temporarily check in a private window.
4. Note the course, page, time, and exact error message.
5. If the issue continues, contact the LMS support team or IT helpdesk.

Problem: Sign-in does not work

1. Check the username and password without sharing credentials or one-time codes.
2. Use the official account-recovery flow if available.
3. If other university services also reject the account, contact the IT helpdesk.
