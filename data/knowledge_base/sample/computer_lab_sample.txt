Title: Computer Lab Troubleshooting (Sample)

Problem: A lab workstation has no network connection

1. Check whether other devices in the same area can connect.
2. Confirm that the workstation's network cable is seated, if a wired connection is used.
3. Restart the browser and retry the affected service.
4. Do not change shared workstation or network settings without authorization.
5. Contact the IT helpdesk with the room, workstation identifier, and approximate time of the issue.

Problem: A shared keyboard, mouse, or monitor is not working

1. Check the cable connections and monitor input selection.
2. If available, test the peripheral on another workstation without changing shared equipment.
3. Report the workstation identifier and the affected device to the IT helpdesk.
