Title: Software Installation Troubleshooting (Sample)

Problem: An installer will not start

1. Check that the installer matches the device's operating system and hardware.
2. Obtain installers only from the software publisher or a source explicitly approved by the organization.
3. Review the displayed error and check the publisher's documented requirements.
4. Do not disable antivirus or other security controls to make an installer run.
5. If administrator permission is requested, contact IT rather than trying to bypass the restriction.
