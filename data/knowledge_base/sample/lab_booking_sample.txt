Title: Lab Booking Troubleshooting (Sample)

Problem: A lab booking request does not complete

1. Confirm that the booking page is the official page provided by the organization.
2. Check that the selected date and time are still shown as available before submitting.
3. Wait for a confirmation message and check the account used to submit the request.
4. Do not assume a booking is confirmed if the page reports an error or no confirmation appears.
5. Contact the lab administrator or IT helpdesk with the time of the request and any error message.
