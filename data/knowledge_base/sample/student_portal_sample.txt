Title: Student Portal Troubleshooting (Sample)

Problem: The portal page does not load

1. Check that the device has an internet connection and reload the page.
2. Confirm that the portal address matches the address provided by the university.
3. Try a current browser or a private window to check whether a browser extension is interfering.
4. If the portal remains unavailable, contact the IT helpdesk and include the time of the issue and any displayed error.

Problem: Sign-in is rejected

1. Check the username and password carefully without sharing them with anyone.
2. Use the portal's official account-recovery option if one is available.
3. If the account may be locked or compromised, contact the IT helpdesk.
